Six models, running live against the index

These are rule-based equity models in live forward testing. Each one is the survivor of successive generations of candidates, selected in historical simulation on U.S. stock data from 1988 to 2026 for a higher annual return than SPY with a maximum drawdown no deeper than the index's over the selection period.

Signals are strictly point-in-time: every decision is computed only from data available at that day's close, and no future input enters any calculation. Parameters and holdings are not disclosed. Technical notes →

Protocol: each account starts with a notional $100,000 in 21 tranches, one tranche entering per trading day. Positions are decided after the close and filled at the next open in whole shares, with 0.05% slippage and no commission. The SPY account buys SPY with the same amounts on the same days. Values refresh every five minutes while the U.S. market is open.

Positions appear under randomly assigned numbers. Symbols, prices and share counts are withheld; gains are rounded to the nearest percent and trade amounts to the nearest $100.

Simulated accounts, no real money. Results in historical simulation do not predict live results. Nothing here is investment advice.
